**Fabrizio De Castelli** is a PhD student in **Computer Science** at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/), with strong interests in graph representation learning, time series, and algorithmic problem solving. His current research focuses on **graph representation learning**, applied to temporal graphs.

Prior to his PhD, he earned his **MSc in Computer Science**, majoring in **Artificial Intelligence** with *Highest Honours* from the University of Pisa in 2025. During this time, he built a solid foundation in deep learning, generative models, and competitive programming. His thesis, *Adaptive Dissipativity in Dynamic Graph Models*, developed in collaboration with the University of Cambridge, explored how dynamical systems, combined with graph representation learning, can ensure an adaptive modulation of information retention/erasure over time and space when handling snapshot-based dynamic graphs.

He earned his **BSc in Computer Science** with *Highest Honours* from the University of Pisa in 2023. During his undergraduate studies, he developed strong skills in algorithms, optimization, and discrete mathematics, skills that continue to guide his research today. His bachelor’s thesis explored an efficient implementation of K-Means in Rust, including a heuristic for cluster splitting that was integrated into a Rust library for fast approximate retrieval. 

Concurrently in 2022–2023, he received the **SEMP Merit Scholarship**, which allowed him to study for one academic year at the [Università della Svizzera italiana (USI)](https://www.usi.ch/en) in Lugano, Switzerland. There, he joined a diverse academic community and deepened his theoretical background in machine learning and data science.

His broader interests include:

- **Probabilistic Models**
- **Competitive programming** 
- **Generative Models**

Fabrizio is open to research collaborations, internships, or meaningful discussions in AI and related areas. Feel free to reach out to him.  

Outside academia, he enjoys playing the guitar, endurance sports, and exploring unseens shorelines.
