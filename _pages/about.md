I’m **Fabrizio De Castelli**, a PhD student in **Computer Science** at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/), with strong interests in graph representation learning, time series, and algorithmic problem solving.

I earned my **MSc in Computer Science**, majoring in **Artificial Intelligence** with *Highest Honours* from the University of Pisa in 2025. During this time I built a solid foundation in deep learning, generative models, and competitive programming. My thesis, *Adaptive Dissipativity in Dynamic Graph Models*, developed in collaboration with the University of Cambridge, explored dynamical systems and dynamic graph representation learning to achieve adaptive dissipation of information over time when handling discrete-time dynamic graphs.

I earned my **BSc in Computer Science** with *Highest Honours* from the University of Pisa in 2023. During my undergraduate studies I developed strong skills in algorithms, optimization, and discrete mathematics (skills that now guide my research). My bachelor’s thesis explored an efficient implementation of K-Means in Rust, including a heuristic for cluster splitting that was integrated into a Rust library for fast approximate retrieval.

In 2022–2023 I received the **SEMP Merit Scholarship**, which allowed me to study at the [Università della Svizzera italiana (USI)](https://www.usi.ch/en) in Lugano, Switzerland. There I joined a diverse academic community and deepened my theoretical background in machine learning and data science.

My current research focuses on **long-range spatiotemporal propagation in dynamic graphs**, where I study effective modeling strategies using tools from dynamical systems.

My broader interests include:

- **Deep learning** (especially geometric and probabilistic models)  
- **Computer vision**  
- **Competitive programming**  
- **Graph representation learning**

I’m open to research collaborations, internships, or meaningful discussions in AI and related areas. Feel free to reach out.

Outside academia, I enjoy playing the guitar, endurance sports, and spending time by the sea.
